Privacy Policy

Need help with Zombreaker, found a bug or have an idea? Write to us. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

E-mail: zombreakersupport@gmail.com

Please include your phone model, its iOS or Android version, the game version and what happened. The game version is shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. The game has no accounts. Uninstalling the game deletes your progress, unless your phone's own backup (for example iCloud) restores it.

How do I change the language?

The game starts in English. Open the game's Settings and pick a language in the Language row.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

Open the game's Settings and switch off Personalized ads. On iPhone you can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads are still shown, but they are not based on your interests.

Why didn't an ad start when I wanted to continue or refill my lives?

Sometimes no ad is available, for example without an internet connection. Try again a little later. Lost lives also come back over time.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game. That removes everything the game stored on your device. Data held by ad partners is explained in the Privacy Policy.
