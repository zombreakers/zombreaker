Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Zombreaker ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (coins, unlocked stages, heroes and weapons, settings, lives) is saved only on your device. We do not receive or store it on our own servers. Your phone's own backup service (for example Android Auto Backup or an iCloud device backup) may include this file. This depends on your phone's settings.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads. You can also choose to watch rewarded ads, for example to continue a run, double your coins or refill your lives. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these companies may collect:

  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size),
  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city),
  • how you interact with ads (views, clicks, installs).

You are asked the first time you open the game whether ads may be personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Personalized ads. If you say no, ads are not based on your interests, but ads are still shown. On iOS, personalized ads also need your permission in Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Each ad partner processes data under its own privacy policy. The list of LevelPlay partners and their policies is available from Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

3. Purchases

If the game offers an optional purchase (for example removing ads), it is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help.

5. Your rights and deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Data collected by ad partners is held by those partners. You can exercise your rights with them directly, or contact us and we will forward your request.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: zombreakersupport@gmail.com
